# BASISForum Functionality Research Notes

## Existing product baseline

BASISForum already has student accounts and profiles, subject threads, two-layer replies, votes, bookmarks, notifications, reporting, moderator workflows, search, campaign management, and an Admin operations dashboard. The strongest gaps are structured learning workflows, durable knowledge curation, student-to-student support programs, and safety settings that go beyond reactive reporting.

## Research signals

| Source | Relevant finding | Product implication |
|---|---|---|
| NC State DELTA, *Discussion Forum Best Practices* | Forums work best when prompts map to learning goals, students have clear participation expectations, and facilitators summarize recurring ideas. | Add structured study prompts, thread goals, and moderator or mentor summaries rather than another generic chat feed. |
| Stanford Teaching Commons, *Online discussion forums* | Student-led moderation and clear participation expectations can support a learning community; selective facilitation can keep discussion on track. | Add topic stewards, answer verification, and summary/next-step controls rather than high-volume engagement mechanics. |
| NGLC, *Peer Learning Pods* | Peer tutoring, feedback, study groups, and student-led sessions are distinct peer-learning patterns; quality control and group dynamics need structure. | Add opt-in study circles and office-hours-style help requests with explicit host roles and reporting controls. |
| NTIA, *Recommended Practices for Industry* | Youth-oriented platforms should default to privacy, avoid engagement-maximizing loops, reduce social-comparison mechanics, and use safety-by-design safeguards. | Avoid DMs, follower counts, streaks, infinite feeds, or targeted ads; prioritize privacy settings, controlled contact, and actionable safety tools. |
| Better Internet for Kids, *Moderation* | Effective youth moderation needs clear harmful-behaviour definitions, rapid action, moderator support, and continuous review. | Add user block/mute, content visibility states, appeal notes, and reason-coded moderator outcomes before expanding social features. |
| Elon Center for Engaged Learning / NASPA mentoring guidance | Mentoring works best with intentional pairing, mentor preparation, boundaries, and ongoing support rather than open-ended matching. | Build a scoped, opt-in academic mentorship directory with subject and grade matching, no default private messages, mentor status, and review checkpoints. |

## Candidate design constraints

Any new functionality should preserve public-by-default academic discussion only when it is safe, use minimal personal data, avoid addictive engagement systems, remain compatible with current role-based moderation, and retain the Swiss editorial UI.
